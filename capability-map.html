🌐 Network Connectivity Capability Model

TOGAF-Aligned Layered Architecture
📁 This is a static visualization. For full documentation, see the repository docs/ folder
1 💼 Business Connectivity Capabilities ▼
Purpose: Define WHY connectivity is needed from a business perspective. Maps to business outcomes and strategic objectives.
Global Presence Connectivity
Connect all business locations worldwide to enable consistent operations across all markets
Customer Access Connectivity
Enable customers to access financial services through any channel, anywhere, anytime
Partner Integration Connectivity
Securely connect to ecosystem partners for extended service delivery
Trading & Markets Connectivity
Ultra-low latency connection to financial markets for competitive advantage
Business Continuity Connectivity
Maintain connectivity during disruptions to meet regulatory requirements
2 🔄 Logical Connectivity Services ▼
Purpose: Define WHAT connectivity services are needed, independent of specific technologies.
Site-to-Site Connectivity Service
Any-to-any connectivity between enterprise locations with predictable performance
Site-to-Cloud Connectivity Service
Private, high-performance connectivity to cloud platforms
User-to-Application Connectivity Service
Seamless user access to applications regardless of location or device
Device-to-Network Connectivity Service
Zero-touch onboarding with automatic policy enforcement
Inter-Cloud Connectivity Service
Private connectivity between different cloud platforms
Market Data Connectivity Service
Ultra-low latency connectivity to trading venues
3 🔌 Physical Connectivity Technologies ▼
Purpose: Define HOW logical services are physically delivered using specific technologies.
MPLS Connectivity
Private label-switched network for predictable enterprise WAN
SD-WAN
Software-defined overlay with intelligent routing
Wi-Fi 6/6E/7
High-performance wireless LAN for campus environments
5G/LTE Cellular
Wide-area wireless for mobility and backup
LEO Satellite
Low-latency satellite for remote locations
Cloud Interconnect
Dedicated private connections to cloud providers
4 🎛️ Connectivity Control Plane ▼
Purpose: Intelligence and automation that makes the network dynamic and self-healing.
Path Selection & Routing
Intelligently determine optimal paths for traffic
Quality of Service Management
Prioritize and manage traffic flows for predictable performance
Connectivity Orchestration
Automate provisioning and lifecycle management
Connectivity Resilience
Maintain connectivity through failures with automatic recovery
5 🛡️ Cross-Cutting Capabilities ▼
Purpose: Capabilities that apply horizontally across ALL layers (Security, Observability, Lifecycle).
Network Access Control
Control what devices and users can connect
Traffic Protection
Protect data confidentiality and integrity in transit
Performance Monitoring
Track connectivity health and application performance
Flow Analytics
Understand traffic patterns and detect anomalies
Capacity Planning
Predict future needs and provision proactively
