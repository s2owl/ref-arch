# Layer 4: Connectivity Control Plane

**Purpose**: Define the intelligence, automation, and orchestration that makes connectivity dynamic and adaptive.

The control plane is what makes modern networks "software-defined" and "intent-based".

---

## Overview

The control plane sits between physical technologies (Layer 3) and services (Layer 2), providing:
- Intelligence (routing decisions, path selection)
- Automation (zero-touch provisioning, auto-remediation)
- Orchestration (service chaining, policy enforcement)
- Resilience (failover, self-healing)

---

## Control Plane Capabilities

### Path Selection & Routing
- Dynamic routing protocols (BGP, OSPF, EIGRP)
- Application-aware routing
- Policy-based routing
- Traffic engineering

### Quality of Service
- Traffic classification
- Queue management
- Policing and shaping
- Bandwidth reservation

### Orchestration & Automation
- Zero-touch provisioning
- Configuration management
- Intent-based networking
- Service chaining

### Resilience & Failover
- Fast failover mechanisms
- Graceful degradation
- Self-healing capabilities
- Health monitoring
