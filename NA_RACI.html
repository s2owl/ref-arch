Capacity Context

As a single-contributor function, the Network Architecture domain cannot feasibly absorb the full range of demand placed upon it. This RACI sets out a deliberate and transparent position: P1 and P2 activities will be delivered; P3 activities will not be delivered without additional resource. This is not a statement of unwillingness — it is a statement of structural capacity.

Where project programmes generate demand that falls within P3, those programmes are expected to fund the Network Architecture resource required to support them. Absorbing this demand without additional resource introduces delivery risk, architectural inconsistency, and an unsustainable operating model.

This document is presented for socialisation and feedback. It does not constitute a refusal of organisational obligation — it constitutes a transparent statement of what is structurally feasible, and an invitation to resolve the gap.
