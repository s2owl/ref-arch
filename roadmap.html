The journey
Now Foundation
Governance Framework live. RACI, conflict resolution, topology conformance, forum model, and process maps published. Governance mandate agreed at GCB3/MD level.
Artefacts Baseline catalogue. ABBs, SBBs, VSBBs, ZBBs, standards, and patterns seeded. Network Capability register live with four initial capabilities.
Measurement Instrumentation in place. GitHub label taxonomy configured. Metrics schema ready. Q1 is the baseline — zero data is the before picture.
Q1–Q2 2026 Adoption
Forum Thread 1 running consistently. CSD bringing work in progress. PA modelling topology conformance challenge. Deviation rate by domain becoming visible.
Data First quarter data. Dwell time diagnosis — capacity constraint or behaviour problem. Retrospective rate shows process adoption pace. Headcount case begins to form.
SASE PROG-001 PoC active. OP-002 (SASE/SD-WAN options paper) progressing. PAT-WAN-002 moves from draft to active on vendor selection. SD-WAN pattern enters the catalogue.
Year 1 Scale
Headcount Evidence-based case made. Two quarters of data. Deviation concentration by domain identifies man-marking targets. Option A or B investment justified by metrics, not assertion.
Patterns Proxy pattern family complete. PAT-PROXY-001 through 005 built and active. Engineering-initiated patterns from CSD in the catalogue. Topology conformance check has real patterns to check against.
Capabilities NC register expanded. NC-005 (DC Interconnect) and additional capability records live. Commercial vs technical availability heatmap surfacing dangerous quadrant findings. DORA resilience reporting enabled.
Year 2 Maturity
ZTNA PROG-003 unblocked. SASE/SD-WAN (PROG-001) complete. ZTNA transition funded and in-flight. VPN retirement path defined. PAT-ZTNA created and active.
Governance ARG/DA pipeline flowing. Options papers endorsed through Thread 2. ARG receiving governed submissions. DA decisions recorded as immutable DEC-XXX records.
Independence Framework operates without PA personally. Patterns, standards, and topology conformance check are clear enough that compliant work moves without PA. PA gates deviations and new capabilities only.
Destination Sustained
Risk Risk is visible and owned. Every deviation documented. Every topology decision traceable. No undocumented artistic licence. Audit trail exists before the auditor asks.
Resilience Contracted capabilities delivered. No dangerous quadrant. Commercial and technical availability aligned across the estate. DORA and PRA obligations demonstrably met.
Independence Not dependent on any individual. The framework governs — not PA personally. It survives changes in personnel, programmes, and technology because it is documented, artefact-driven, and structurally independent.
