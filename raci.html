Governance mandate: PA operates under independent architecture governance authority, separate from the networks service delivery organisation. PA is peer grade to GCB3. No shared MD. Unresolved conflicts between PA and GCB3 escalate to respective MDs. The co-reviewed-by field in governance/conflict-resolution.yaml must be signed by the networks GCB3 before this framework is operational.
